### 1. What is Agentic AI in your own words?
Agentic AI is a type of AI that can think, decide, and take actions on its own to complete a task.

### 2. How is Agentic AI different from a normal chatbot?
A normal chatbot only answers questions using stored knowledge. Agentic AI can use tools, fetch real-time data, and perform tasks automatically.

### 3. One real-life example where Agentic AI can help humans
Example: A time assistant that tells the current time in any city when asked, by fetching real-time data from an API.
